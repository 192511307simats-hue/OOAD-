🔎 OFFICIAL CASE SOLUTION

Arjun Mehta, the System Administrator, stole the prototype. He had the required administrator privileges and his access card was used to enter the laboratory at 8:27 PM. The prototype was exported to an external device at 8:31 PM. Arjun claimed he had left before 8:00 PM, but the access record directly contradicts his statement.
